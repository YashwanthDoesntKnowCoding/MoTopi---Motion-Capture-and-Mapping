📱 Use Your Phone as the MoCap Camera

To use your iPhone with MoTopi running on your Windows PC, first connect it through a webcam app. Once Windows recognizes the iPhone as a camera, select it from the camera dropdown.

Recommended • iPhone + Windows

1. Camo (USB or Wi-Fi)

  1. Install Camo Studio on Windows and Camo Camera from the App Store on your iPhone.
  2. Open both apps, allow camera access on the iPhone, then connect by USB or follow Camo's Wi-Fi pairing instructions.
  3. Keep Camo Camera open. In MoTopi, select Camo (or the iPhone camera name) from the camera dropdown.
Alternative

2. Iriun Webcam

  1. Install Iriun Webcam on Windows and its camera app on your iPhone.
  2. Open both apps and connect using the same Wi-Fi network or USB, following Iriun's setup prompts.
  3. Choose Iriun Webcam from MoTopi's camera dropdown.
💡 Pro MoCap Tips:
  • Place your phone in landscape mode on a shelf or tripod around chest/hip height.
  • For full-body tracking, keep your head through feet visible. Use the body calibration control for more stable estimates of briefly hidden joints.
  • USB usually gives the most reliable connection. The available frame rate depends on the iPhone, connection, and webcam app.
  • If the camera does not appear, keep the iPhone app open, reconnect it, and reload MoTopi.
